Feed with your ADSBee

New ADSBee 1090s feed whereplane out of the box. To check:

1Get online

Join Wi-Fi or plug in Ethernet.

AT+WIFI_STA=1,<ssid>,<password>

2Check feed 6

Look for {{ feed_host }}, port 30004, BEAST, active.

AT+FEED?

3Add it if missing

AT+FEED=6,{{ feed_host }},30004,1,BEASTAT+SETTINGS=SAVE

Or in the web console: Feeds, add, Beast.

4Find your bee

AT+FEED_RECEIVER_ID? shows its ID; the console's feed card links to its traffic. Then look it up in Feeders.

whereplane is for ADSBees: please don't point other receivers here. Thanks!
